title: Reference
---

The complete public surface of the plugin, enumerated from source. Every route, hook, block, pattern, and command listed here exists in the current release; nothing is planned or aspirational.

- **[REST API](rest-api.md)** lists the five public routes and five administrator routes under `psst/v1`, with request schemas, response bodies, and every error code.
- **[Hooks](hooks.md)** lists the ten actions and nineteen filters, their arguments, defaults, and the floors applied after filtering.
- **[Blocks and patterns](blocks-and-patterns.md)** covers the two blocks, their attributes and states, the two patterns, the block binding source, and how styling follows the theme.
- **[Development](development.md)** covers the two npm projects, the Composer gates, the unit and end-to-end suites, the local Playground, and the release build.

Settings keys and constants are documented in [Configuration](../getting-started/configuration.md).
